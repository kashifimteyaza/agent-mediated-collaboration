§ Position provocation · Nobody Did This, CSCW 2026

Where Does the Disagreement Go? Group Reflexivity and AI Accounts of Team Decision-Making

Soohwan Lee · Kyungho Lee · UNIST, Republic of Korea

Download PDF All accepted papers
§ Abstract

Position paper

AI agents mediate not only individual contributions but also the process through which many contributions become a team position. This matters when an AI-generated account of a meeting reaches another team, a manager, or an organization. Such an account may be accurate sentence by sentence while obscuring how the group reached its position, whose contributions shaped it, and which differences remain unresolved. We develop this position through two scenarios in which an account loses something: apparent consensus that leaves a concern unresolved, and a contribution that moves across speech and shared artifacts. We then present a counter-case in which an agent makes stakeholder positions visible, and that visibility may harden the divisions it was meant to clarify. Taken together, the cases suggest that the design problem is not how much an account should show, but whether a group can examine the interpretation it carries. We therefore ask how agents might scaffold members in tracing contributions and differences before an account travels, and we consider evaluating such support by whether a group, including members who brought different perspectives, can explain how it reached its decision, rather than by whether most members approve the account.

Keywords: agent-mediated collaboration, group reflexivity, contribution attribution, accountability, collective account, meeting summarization
