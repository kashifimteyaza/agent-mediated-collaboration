§ Position provocation · Nobody Did This, CSCW 2026

Only I Will Remain: How AI Turns Team Contributions into One Collective Voice

Diego Gómez-Zará · University of Notre Dame, Notre Dame, IN, USA

SSRN All accepted papers
§ Abstract

Position paper

As AI systems increasingly synthesize team discussions and contributions, they can make a group appear to speak with one voice before its members have developed that voice together. We use contribution dissolution to describe this transformation: individual inputs are reorganized into a shared representation in ways that can obscure how those contributions differed, related to one another, and shaped the resulting position. Such synthesis may strengthen group thinking by surfacing overlooked ideas, connecting distributed knowledge, and generating interpretations that no member produced alone. At the same time, it may smooth productive tensions, encourage premature convergence, and allow one generated interpretation to stand in for the team as a whole. We outline design directions for collaborative AI systems that help teams examine alternative interpretations, recover overlooked possibilities, and decide whether an AI-generated representation is one they are prepared to adopt as their own.
