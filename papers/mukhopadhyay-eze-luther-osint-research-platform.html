OSINT Research Platform as Accountability Infrastructure for Collaborative Sensemaking

Anirban Mukhopadhyay · Kelechi Eze · Kurt Luther · Virginia Tech

Download PDF All accepted papers
§ Abstract

Position paper

As AI agents move from answering questions to observing teams, calling tools, and proposing next steps, it becomes difficult to say what the agent contributed, and how the team leveraged those contributions in their final result. Here, we present the OSINT Research Platform (ORP) as a design provocation for accountability infrastructure. ORP is a shared workspace for small teams conducting Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) vulnerability assessments. A browser extension provides lightweight team context; a tool-calling LLM agent uses a bounded set of passive OSINT tools; and the interface exposes plans, tool calls, results, sources, and proactive suggestions. Our provocation is that accountability should be designed into the division of labor while work unfolds, rather than reconstructed from a final output or an AI-use log. ORP shows how systems can observe team members, record it, and provide more contextual support and metadata. However, these observations create privacy, surveillance, and interpretive risks.

Keywords: Human–AI collaboration; Accountability; OSINT; Context-Aware Agents
